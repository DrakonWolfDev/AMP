AMP distribution licence index (LicenseRef-AMP-Distribution)

This reference identifies the collection of original licence terms applying to
an upstream AMP binary distribution. It does not create a new licence, change
any component's licence, or grant patent rights.

AMP first-party code: AGPL-3.0-only; see LICENSE.

The other components retain their original licences. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md
identifies their versions, sources, selected licensing routes and preserved
licence files. The LICENSES directory includes full retained licence texts,
ICU's release licence, Qt Image Formats' incorporated third-party notices,
and, in frozen builds, CPython and PyInstaller licences. Installed distribution
metadata in the frozen runtime retains additional component licence files.

The published corresponding-source bundle preserves the Qt module attribution
and licence records and exact source inputs listed in legal/source-manifest.json.
See docs/QT_REDISTRIBUTION.md and docs/SOURCE_DELIVERY.md for the scope and
replacement/source-delivery arrangements.

The Linux distribution supplies libmpv, codecs and other excluded system
libraries separately. Their licence terms remain applicable to those packages.
This index is for upstream distribution metadata; it is not a Fedora package
collection licence approval or a claim of codec-patent clearance.
